Privacy

Beyond STEP is a free book. You do not need an account to read it, and the site does not ask for or store any personal information.

Analytics

If you agree, the site uses Google Analytics (measurement ID G-M8RDPR4MXR) to count visits: which pages are read, which PDFs and worksheets are downloaded, what is searched for on the site, and roughly where visitors come from (country, device type, the site that linked here). Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser to do this. The information is used only to understand which parts of the book are useful and to improve it; it is not used for advertising, and it is not combined with any other data.

UK law (the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations and the UK GDPR) requires consent before non-essential cookies such as analytics cookies are set, which is why the site asks first. Nothing is collected unless you choose “Accept”. If you choose “Reject”, or make no choice, the Google Analytics script is never loaded and no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is remembered in your browser’s local storage, so that you are not asked on every page.

You can change your mind at any time with the cookie settings link at the foot of every page; withdrawing consent removes the analytics cookies. Google’s own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web server, records basic technical information such as IP addresses in its server logs for security purposes. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Mathematical formulae are displayed with MathJax, and the search page uses the MiniSearch library; both are loaded from the jsDelivr content delivery network.
