Beyond STEP

Questions by Paper

Every STEP question in the book, from 1987 to 2025, listed by paper and year.

Each question links to the section that solves it: a guided solution, the university mathematics behind it, and a printable worksheet. A question marked also in is not the section’s main question but is worked through or set as an exercise there. Specimen means the specimen papers issued before the first STEP in 1987.
