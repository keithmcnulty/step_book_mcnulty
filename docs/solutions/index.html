Worked Solutions

A worked solution to every STEP question from 1987 to 2025, each a printable PDF.

Have a proper go at a question before you open its solution: STEP rewards the struggle, and a solution read too early teaches much less than one read after an hour of trying. Each solution starts with the key idea, so you can read just that and go back to the question.

The questions themselves are not reproduced here. Each Question link goes to the official source: the STEP Support Programme’s question database for papers up to 2018, and OCR’s downloads (zip files of the year’s papers) from 2019. Where a question has a full guided treatment in the book, the § link takes you to that section, with hints, a worksheet and the university mathematics behind it.

Every answer has been checked numerically, but with this many solutions some slips are inevitable: if something looks wrong, trust your own working and check it carefully. Corrections are very welcome as GitHub issues.
